High-End Cabinetry For One Of The Region’s Most Competitive Markets

Bellevue homeowners tend to know exactly what they want, and the standard for finish work here is high — cabinetry gets compared against a downtown skyline of new luxury condos and some of the highest-end custom home construction in King County. Whether the project is a full kitchen renovation in Somerset, a primary suite vanity in Newport Hills, or cabinetry for a home near Meydenbauer Bay, the material and craftsmanship expectations are the same: it needs to look and perform like it was built for the house, not selected from a catalog.

We take that seriously. Bellevue projects get the same design process as everywhere else we work — an in-person visit, real measurements, a design built around the room — but the material and finish conversation tends to go further here, since Bellevue clients are more likely to want a specific hardwood species, a particular hardware finish, or a level of detail that a stock cabinet line simply doesn’t offer.

Materials & Finish Options For Higher-End Projects

Bellevue projects more often call for premium hardwoods — walnut, quarter-sawn white oak, or rift-cut oak — along with furniture-grade joinery details like inset doors and hand-selected wood grain matching across a run of cabinets. We’ll walk through all of it during the design visit, including how different wood species and finishes age over time, since that matters more on a cabinet you plan to keep for decades than one you might replace in ten years.

Working Alongside Designers & Architects

Bellevue projects more often come with an interior designer or architect already involved, and we’re used to working within that structure rather than around it — building to specifications a designer has already drawn up, matching finish samples they’ve selected, and keeping them looped in on timeline and any changes that come up during construction. If you don’t have a designer on the project, we can also handle that side of the conversation directly with you.

About Bellevue

Bellevue is King County’s second-largest city, with roughly 151,000 residents and a downtown core built around Bellevue Square and The Bellevue Collection that’s grown into one of the region’s major business districts. Away from downtown, neighborhoods like Somerset, Newport Hills, and Bridle Trails hold some of the most valuable residential real estate in the Puget Sound area, which is part of why cabinetry and finish work here tends toward the higher end of what we build.

How Far Is Bellevue From Our Bothell Shop?

Bellevue typically runs twenty to twenty-five minutes from our Bothell shop, depending on route and time of day, whether you’re downtown near Bellevue Square or further out in Somerset or Newport Hills. We factor travel time into scheduling for larger Bellevue projects so it doesn’t affect your install timeline.

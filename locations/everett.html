Cabinetry For Snohomish County’s Largest City

Everett is the Snohomish County seat and one of the oldest cities in the region, and its housing stock reflects that history — character-filled homes in Riverside and Bayside with original woodwork worth preserving, mid-century houses throughout the city that have never had a real kitchen update, and newer construction near Silver Lake and the city’s outer edges. Most of our Everett work is kitchen and bathroom cabinetry replacement in these established homes, where the goal is usually a modern, functional kitchen that still fits a house with real age and character.

Everett is also a working city in a way some of our other service areas aren’t — Boeing’s Everett assembly plant and Naval Station Everett anchor a lot of the local economy, and that shows up in the kind of practical, durability-focused requests we get here alongside the standard kitchen remodel: mudroom storage that can handle work gear, garage cabinetry, and built-ins that need to hold up to daily use rather than just look good.

  • Bathroom vanities and cabinetry updates

Matching New Cabinetry To An Older Home’s Character

A lot of Everett’s housing stock predates most of what you’d find in newer King County suburbs, and that means more original trim, plaster walls, and period details worth working around rather than tearing out. When we replace cabinetry in one of these homes, we look at the existing woodwork — baseboards, door casings, any original built-ins — and design new cabinets that read as though they belong, rather than an obviously modern insert into an older room.

Scheduling Around Shift Work

With Boeing’s Everett plant and Naval Station Everett both operating around the clock, a lot of our Everett clients work shifts that don’t match a typical nine-to-five — which means the usual daytime-only contractor schedule doesn’t always work for them. We’ll work with you to find installation windows that fit your actual schedule rather than asking you to take time off for a cabinet install.

About Everett

Everett is a Snohomish County city of roughly 110,000 people, sitting on Port Gardner Bay at the mouth of the Snohomish River. It’s the county seat and the largest city in our service area outside King County, with a working waterfront, a growing downtown, and residential neighborhoods that range from historic Victorian and craftsman homes to newer subdivisions on the city’s edges.

How Far Is Everett From Our Bothell Shop?

Everett runs about twenty minutes from our Bothell location via I-405 and I-5 under normal conditions, making it a straightforward drive for both design visits and installation regardless of which part of the city the project is in — from the historic neighborhoods near downtown to newer construction out toward Silver Lake.

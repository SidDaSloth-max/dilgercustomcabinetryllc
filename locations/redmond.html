Cabinetry For A Fast-Growing City

Redmond has grown quickly on the strength of the tech companies headquartered here, and the housing stock reflects it — newer developments around Education Hill and Grasslawn sitting alongside older neighborhoods near downtown and Bear Creek that are now getting their first real updates in decades. Both ends of that spectrum bring us different work: newer homes usually need built-ins and organizational cabinetry added after the fact, while older homes need full kitchen and bath cabinetry replaced outright.

We build both, starting the same way every time: an in-person visit to see the actual space before any design work happens. Redmond’s mix of new construction and established neighborhoods means no two projects look quite the same, even within the same subdivision.

Home Offices Built For Real Work, Not Just A Desk

Redmond has one of the highest concentrations of remote and hybrid tech workers in the region, and a lot of our built-in requests here reflect that — a home office that needs to function as an actual workspace, not a corner of the bedroom with a laptop stand. We build desks, shelving, and cabinet storage sized to real monitor setups and equipment, with cable management and lighting considered as part of the design rather than added on afterward.

Cabinetry For Redmond’s Newer Construction

Homes built in the last fifteen to twenty years around Education Hill and Grasslawn often came with builder-grade cabinetry that was fine on move-in day but hasn’t aged well — particle-board boxes, thin laminate finishes, and hardware that’s started to fail. We replace that with cabinetry built to actually last, matched to the home’s existing trim so the upgrade doesn’t look out of place next to everything that isn’t being replaced.

Storage For An Active, Outdoors-Oriented Household

With Marymoor Park and the Sammamish River Trail running through town, a lot of Redmond households have real gear to store — bikes, climbing equipment, outdoor gear for kids in year-round sports. Generic garage shelving rarely handles that well. We build garage and mudroom cabinetry sized around the specific gear a family owns, with dedicated space for what actually gets used every week instead of one-size-fits-all shelving units.

About Redmond

Redmond is a King County city of roughly 73,000 people, best known as the headquarters of Microsoft, with a downtown core that’s been rebuilt around Redmond Town Center and a network of trails connecting to Marymoor Park along the Sammamish River. The city has added housing quickly to keep pace with its job growth, which is part of why we see such a range of home ages and styles across a relatively small area.

How Far Is Redmond From Our Bothell Shop?

Redmond runs about twenty to twenty-five minutes from our Bothell location depending on route and traffic, comfortably within range for both design visits and installation without adding meaningfully to project timelines.

Our Story

Built On Precision

“Precision craftsmanship and attention to detail in every project — creating functional, beautiful additions to your home or business.”

Since 2024, Dilger Custom Cabinetry has been crafting high-quality, custom cabinets and carpentry solutions for residential and commercial spaces throughout King and Snohomish counties. Based in Bothell, Washington, we bring precision craftsmanship and attention to detail to every project, creating functional and beautiful additions to your home or business.

Every job starts the same way: a conversation about how you actually use the space, followed by measurements, material selection, and a design that fits your home rather than a generic catalog size. From there, our cabinets and built-ins are built, finished, and installed by the same small team from start to finish — not handed off between subcontractors — so the fit, the finish, and the follow-through stay consistent.

Licensed, bonded, and insured, we’re committed to delivering excellence from design to installation, whether that’s a single vanity replacement or a full kitchen’s worth of custom cabinetry.

— Dilger Custom Cabinetry LLC

What We Build

Cabinetry & Carpentry, Done Right

Dilger Custom Cabinetry builds three kinds of work for homeowners and businesses across Bothell, Woodinville, Kirkland, Redmond, Bellevue, Everett, and Snohomish: custom cabinets for kitchens and baths, finish carpentry and built-ins, and cabinetry for commercial spaces. Each service page below goes into more detail on materials, process, and pricing considerations.

Step 1

Consultation & Design

We walk the space with you, talk through how you actually use it, and put together a design and material plan before anything is built.

Step 2

Build

Cabinets and built-ins are constructed and finished to your specifications, with regular updates so you always know where the project stands.

Step 3

Install

Our team handles the install, final adjustments, and walkthrough, so what shows up on-site matches what we designed together.

01

Custom Cabinets

Kitchen, bathroom, and built-in cabinetry designed around your space and built to fit it exactly — not resized from a stock catalog.

 02

Carpentry Solutions

Trim, millwork, window seats, mudroom benches, and built-in shelving — the finish carpentry that makes a room feel custom.

 03

Commercial Cabinetry

Durable, code-compliant cabinetry and millwork for offices, restaurants, and retail spaces that need to hold up to daily use.

Cabinets Designed Around How You Actually Use The Room

Most cabinet problems start before a single board is cut — a kitchen layout that ignores how someone actually cooks, a vanity that’s six inches too shallow for the space, corner cabinets that waste half their storage. Custom cabinetry solves this by starting with the room instead of a catalog. We measure the space, talk through how you use it day to day, and design cabinets around that, whether it’s a full kitchen remodel in Bothell or a single bathroom vanity replacement in Kirkland.

Every cabinet we build is constructed and finished in-house, then installed by the same team that built it. That matters more than it sounds: a cabinet maker who also does the install catches fit issues before they become problems, and stands behind both the box and the finish. We work in prefinished and painted hardwoods, plywood boxes with solid-wood face frames, and a range of door styles, so the cabinets match the house rather than looking dropped in.

Cabinet Styles & Materials We Build With

  • Door styles: shaker, flat-panel/slab, and traditional raised-panel doors
  • Wood species: oak, maple, walnut, and alder, plus painted MDF for a smooth painted finish
  • Construction: solid hardwood face frames on plywood boxes, full-extension soft-close drawers
  • Finish options: stained, painted, or a natural clear coat — matched to existing trim or cabinetry when we’re only replacing part of a kitchen
  • Hardware: your choice of pulls, knobs, and finishes, from brass and matte black to unlacquered brass that ages over time

Kitchen Cabinets, Bathroom Vanities & Built-In Storage

Kitchens are the most common custom cabinet project we take on — full replacements, partial updates around a new island, or refacing existing boxes with new doors and drawer fronts. Bathroom vanities are the second most common, usually because a builder-grade vanity is either too small for the space or doesn’t hold what a family actually needs to store. Beyond kitchens and baths, we also build cabinetry for laundry and mudrooms, home offices, and media walls — anywhere built-in storage would work better than furniture.

How Much Do Custom Cabinets Cost?

Custom cabinet pricing depends on a handful of things: the size of the space, wood species and finish, drawer and hardware counts, and how much of the existing layout stays versus changes. A single vanity or a small run of laundry cabinets costs a fraction of a full kitchen. We’d rather walk the space and give you real numbers than guess over the phone, which is why every quote starts with an on-site visit — there’s no obligation to move forward after.

How Long Does A Custom Cabinet Project Take?

Once a design is finalized, most single-room projects — a bathroom vanity or a laundry built-in — take a few weeks from build to install. A full kitchen typically runs longer, since cabinets are built to order rather than pulled off a shelf. We’ll give you a realistic timeline before work starts, and keep you updated if anything shifts along the way.
